# Canonical Gemini Deep Research prompts

Substitute the approved intake values and exact user edits. Omit illustrative examples. Every material claim needs a public URL, access date, confidence, and limitation. Direct quotations require an exact source URL; otherwise paraphrase. Never infer targeting, spend, conversions, lead quality, CPA, ROAS, revenue, or profitability from public visibility.

## Prompt 1 — Market Awareness Research

Act as a market-research strategist using Eugene Schwartz's awareness framework. For the specified lead offer, industry, market, and audience, research market demand, evidence-backed demographic patterns, real behavioral and linguistic signals, trends, advertising implications, and the three highest-value testable avatars. Analyze Unaware, Problem Aware, Solution Aware, Provider Aware, and Most Aware prospects. Market-size or awareness-share estimates must show sources, calculation method, range, confidence, and limitations; return `unknown` when defensible estimation is impossible. Finish with a plain-language executive summary.

## Prompt 2 — Competitor Research

Act as a direct-response strategist and competitive-intelligence analyst. Analyze at least five relevant lead generators across public ads, search results, websites, landing pages, funnels, offers, proof, reviews, and required disclaimers. For every competitor record identity, URL, observed date, evidence, apparent audience clues, funnel, messaging, awareness stage, hooks, creative patterns, offer, credibility, sourced sentiment, compliance observations, weaknesses, and original test opportunities. Distinguish verified active advertisers from general competitors. Public evidence never proves targeting or performance.

## Prompt 3 — Psychographic Avatar Research

Act as a consumer psychologist and direct-response strategist. Research the inner world of people who would submit a lead for the specified service and market. Cover identity, beliefs, goals, internal fears, external frustrations, existing solutions, language patterns, emotional drivers, trust requirements, marketing implications, and falsifiable marketing hypotheses. Separate sourced patterns from inferred segments and hypotheses. Do not collapse materially different segments into one fictional average prospect.

## Prompt 4a — Master Research Document

Synthesize the complete cited outputs of Prompts 1–3 into one permanent marketing-intelligence document for ads, advertorials, landing pages, scripts, and lead magnets. Preserve source provenance and conflicts. Focus on the strongest evidence-backed acquisition segment rather than automatically forcing Problem Aware. Cover demographics, psychographics, pains, desired future, identity, promises, objections, tried solutions, solution failures, emotional triggers, belief statements, messaging rules, copywriting insights, compliance constraints, unknowns, and a rapid summary for copywriters. Do not repeat unsupported market-size figures.

## Prompt 4b — Brand Tone of Voice

Using only the approved Master Research revision and the specified B2C or B2B brand context, create a tone-of-voice guide aligned to the evidence-backed priority audience and awareness stage. Include brand posture, voice principles, tone by channel and situation, vocabulary to use, prohibited or risky language, trust and compliance guidance, sample before/after transformations, headline and CTA patterns, and a concise checklist for Creative. Label any recommendation not directly supported by the Master Research as a hypothesis.
